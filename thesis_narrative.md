While temporary emotional shifts, acute stress, or surge adrenaline cause immediate micro-fluctuations in the active bandwidth, the core underlying baseline signature remains unalterable. 

The ultimate layer of precision in this system is anchored at the genetic level. Human DNA strands inherently act as microscopic fractal antennas. Due to their double-helix geometry and electrical conductivity, chromosomes interact with and emit specific electromagnetic wave profiles. By utilizing non-invasive, high-sensitivity quantum magnetometers alongside localized ultrasonic acoustic transducers, the system reads the live harmonic output of an individual’s genetic structures passively. This completely eliminates the need for legacy chemical sequencing or active user compliance; identity is verified purely through the physical presence of living tissue.

### Data Governance and Privacy Sovereignty

To withstand rigorous regulatory scrutiny and comply with modern data privacy mandates, the framework rejects the use of centralized master databases. Raw biological frequencies are never permanently stored or transmitted across open networks. Instead, the real-time wave telemetry is processed at the edge and instantly encrypted into a unique, one-way 256-bit alphanumeric hash string. If a network node is breached, the data is entirely useless to a malicious actor, as a physical human frequency cannot be reverse-engineered from a static digital hash file. This ensures absolute privacy sovereignty while maintaining airtight authentication integrity.
