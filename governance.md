# Frequency Privacy & Governance Blueprint

As frequency-reading devices become portable and passive, humanity faces an unprecedented threat to absolute privacy. This document outlines the foundational legal protections required before deployment.

## ⚖️ The Three Pillars of Frequency Law

### 1. The Right to Frequency Anonymity
* **Rule**: Public infrastructure cannot scan ambient spaces to identify citizens without an active, judicial warrant.
* **Intent**: Prevents governments or corporate entities from tracking protests, religious gatherings, or daily commutes in total darkness.

### 2. Physical Data Decoupling
* **Rule**: Raw frequency data cannot be converted into a centralized digital master file.
* **Intent**: If a security database is breached, hackers must only see randomized, encrypted hash strings, making it impossible to "re-create" or target an individual's physical frequency.

### 3. Medical Frequency Sovereignty
* **Rule**: Employers and insurance companies are strictly banned from scanning a person's baseline frequency to check for underlying health conditions, cellular anomalies, or stress levels.
* **Intent**: Prevents systematic discrimination based on real-time biological data.
